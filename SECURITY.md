# Security policy

Please do not report suspected vulnerabilities in a public issue or discussion.

Open a [private vulnerability report](https://github.com/c6shq/feedback/security/advisories/new)
with:

- the affected c6s or Cerberus surface and version;
- reproducible steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- the security impact you observed;
- any suggested mitigation; and
- a safe way to contact you.

Do not include real credentials, recovery material, private keys, access tokens, or
another person's data. Use synthetic test values and redact logs before attaching them.

Only organization maintainers can see a private report. We will acknowledge it when
received and coordinate remediation and disclosure privately. A response target is
not guaranteed during the private beta.
