# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and mindful that security and access problems can be
stressful. Focus feedback on observable behavior and give others room to clarify.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private data,
and attempts to obtain another person's credentials are not accepted.

Report conduct concerns privately to **security@whitekiwi.link**. Maintainers may edit,
hide, or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
